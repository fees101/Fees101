Who can do what

Twenty-one permissions across four roles — the densest surface in the product. The catalog splits in two: nine SEE keys govern what a role can look at, twelve DO keys govern what it can change. Owner bypasses every switch and cannot be edited.

Roles are the school's own, not ours. There is no fixed set — a school creates as many as it needs, and each one is these same 21 switches. The four below are only what a new school is seeded with.

Owner is a bypass, not a column of grants. A role flagged as owner clears every check regardless of its switches, so its dots are shown hollow — they are not doing the work.

Changes land on the next click. Permissions are read per request, so a switch flipped here reaches that person without them signing out.

Read down a column to understand a role, across a row to see who holds a power. A filled dot is granted, a dash is not.
