Program Foundations App

The only surface families ever see.

Six hundred and forty parents never open the app. They get an SMS and an email — so these four artefacts are Fees101 to them, and they're the highest-traffic thing in the product by an order of magnitude.

Each is shown as it exists today, drawn from composeInvoice.ts and reminders.ts, beside the rework. The constraints are kept exactly: email stays table-based and client-safe, SMS stays GSM-7 with "NGN" rather than ₦, and inside one 160-character segment.

What changed, and why

Amount first, greeting last

An SMS preview shows roughly 40 characters. Today every one of them is spent on "Hello Mr Adebayo Kunle, this is the…" — the parent must open the message to learn anything. Leading with the amount makes it answerable from the lock screen.

Overdue reads as overdue

Today the only difference between the due and overdue reminder is "are due" versus "were due" — two characters. The rework states how many days late and what happens next, because a reminder that doesn't escalate gets ignored on the third send.

Headroom for real names

With these short sample names every current template already fits one segment — but the invoice sits at exactly 160, with none to spare. A longer parent, student or school name (the cap is 30 characters) pushes it to 208 and bills twice. The rework leaves 33 characters of slack, so it holds for every family on the roster.

The school stays the sender

The school name leads every message and Fees101 is never named in the body — parents have a relationship with the school, not the platform. The sender ID already carries the attribution.
